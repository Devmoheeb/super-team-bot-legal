Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This policy describes information that may be processed when the SUPER TEAM Discord bot (“the Bot”) is used in a Discord server. The exact information processed depends on which features the server administrator enables and which version is installed.

Server-owner controlled hosting: In the described local setup, the Bot and its dashboard run on the operator’s computer. Data written to local log, case, configuration, and backup files is controlled by the server/Bot operator. Do not assume every deployment has the same storage setup.

1. Information the Bot may process

  • Discord identifiers: user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and message or interaction identifiers needed to perform commands and configure features.
  • Moderation and security events: event details available through Discord, including channel/role changes, bans, kicks, permission changes, bot additions, and relevant audit-log entries.
  • Messages and attachments: message text, links, and attachment metadata may be checked to enforce anti-spam, link, image, and profanity rules. Processing depends on enabled filters and Discord permissions/intents.
  • Invite and membership events: invite codes and use counts, and join/departure details used for invite attribution, welcome cards, and activity logs where Discord exposes the relevant information.
  • Moderation records: warnings, case records, timeout/ban action details, and information administrators choose to include in notes.
  • Server structure and backups: role/channel names, IDs, settings and other supported structural information saved for backup comparison, restore, or clone features.
  • Music requests: search terms and submitted YouTube or Spotify URLs needed to resolve and play requested music.

2. How information is used

The Bot uses this information to provide moderation and security features, detect and report activity, maintain logs, track invites, render welcome cards, manage cases, create and compare backups, execute administrator commands, run the local dashboard, and resolve music requests.

3. Storage and retention

Depending on configuration, information may be stored in local files or backup snapshots on the machine running the Bot, and in Discord messages used for logs or reports. Automatic backup retention may be configurable (the described setup defaults to retaining up to 30 automatic snapshots); other log and case retention depends on the operator’s settings and cleanup practices. The server/Bot operator is responsible for securing the computer, backups, credentials, and log channels.

4. Sharing and third-party services

The Bot communicates with Discord to operate. Music search/playback may rely on third-party services or libraries that fetch public metadata or resolve links from YouTube or Spotify. Requests to those services may reveal the submitted URL or search terms to the relevant provider, under that provider’s policies. The Bot operator should review third-party dependencies and their behavior. We do not claim ownership of, affiliation with, or endorsement by Discord, YouTube, or Spotify.

5. Access controls and security

The local dashboard in the described configuration listens on 127.0.0.1, which limits direct access to the same computer. This does not replace a strong dashboard password, secure storage of the Discord bot token, operating-system protections, and appropriate permissions on private logging channels. Do not expose the dashboard to the public internet without a properly designed authentication and network-security setup.

6. User choices and requests

Server administrators can disable features, adjust log access and retention, remove the Bot from their server, or delete local files under their control. For a question or deletion request concerning records held by the operator, contact the SUPER TEAM server administrators through the Discord community where the Bot is used. Some records may need to be retained for moderation, security, or legal reasons. Discord separately processes account data under its own privacy policy.

7. Children and sensitive information

Do not use the Bot to collect sensitive personal information or to create unnecessary profiles of members. Server administrators should configure their communities and data practices in accordance with Discord’s rules and applicable law.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Bot’s features or data practices change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the SUPER TEAM server administrators through the official Discord community where the Bot is provided. Add a monitored contact email here if one becomes available.
